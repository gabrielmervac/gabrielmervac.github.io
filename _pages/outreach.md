## Clubes de Ciencia

Co-coordinator of the science-outreach program **“Clubes de Ciencia,”** funded by the University of Chicago, bringing intensive, hands-on science workshops to students and connecting them with active researchers.

- **2026** — Co-coordinator, Clubes de Ciencia (University of Chicago)
- **2025** — Co-coordinator, Clubes de Ciencia (University of Chicago)
